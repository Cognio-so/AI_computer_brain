## Agent Notes

Belongs here: strategy, pricing and packaging, shipped features, sprint priorities, open or recently made decisions.
Does not belong here: brand rules (DESIGN_SYSTEM.md), technical details (TECH_AND_INFRA.md), schema (DB_SCHEMA_STATE.md).
A section whose only line starts with "(no" is empty: it holds no facts, so never quote a price, a feature or a priority from it. Ask the admin or the owning department, then replace the placeholder line with the facts.
Keep Shipped Recently to the last ten items, newest first. Move resolved decisions out of Open Decisions with a one-line outcome.
